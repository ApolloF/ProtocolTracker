Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.3.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

This release imports lab results from a lab report with the help of an AI chatbot, shows bloodwork and blood pressure over time, and records injection sites.

## Import lab results (dev build)
1. **Open the import.** On Today, tap Log › Bloodwork, then **Import results** at the top of the sheet (Journal's "+" › Bloodwork works too).
2. **Copy the prompt.** Tap **Copy AI prompt**.
3. **Ask a chatbot.** In any chatbot, paste the prompt and add your lab report: a PDF, a photo or the text. Your report goes to the chatbot you use; the app stays offline.
4. **Copy the answer.** Tap **Copy** on the chatbot's answer, not Share (Share makes a public link to the chat, which the app can't read).
5. **Paste it.** Come back to the app and tap **Paste answer**. If the answer can't be read, one line says why. If it was cut off, a note on the next screen says so: ask the chatbot to write the whole answer again, then copy it.
6. **Check.** "Check results" shows one card per blood draw, newest first, with its date, time and lab. Each row has the app's marker name, the name as printed, the value and unit as printed, and Low, High or In range by the lab's own range when the report gives one. Tests the app does not list are kept as printed (they appear under "Other tests").
   - Anything uncertain is left out, struck through, with the reason. A tap on a row leaves it out or keeps it.
   - "N not imported" and "N already saved" fold open to show those rows with their reasons.
   - A draw whose date is unclear is left out as a whole. A draw without a printed time is saved at 12:00; you can correct the time in the Bloodwork sheet afterwards.
7. **Save.** Tap **Save N results** (or **Save N draws**). Nothing is saved before this. Journal opens on the Bloodwork chip with "Bloodwork saved" and **Undo**. Back before saving asks "Discard this import?".

The saved draws are normal bloodwork entries: you can edit them, they appear in reports and backups, and total testosterone shows on the Testosterone level curve. Pasting the same answer again saves nothing twice.

## Bloodwork and blood pressure over time (dev build)
- **Last draw.** The Bloodwork row of Today's Log menu reads "Last draw 3 days ago" (in days, then weeks, then months), and the Bloodwork card in Journal reads "Bloodwork · last draw 3 days ago".
- **One marker over time.** Tap a row on the Bloodwork card in Journal. A sheet shows a chart once there are two or more results, shaded with the lab range of the latest result (or the typical adult male range), and every result below it, newest first, with date and lab. Results with "<" or ">" are listed but not plotted. Tests the app does not list come after the others (those without a Low or High behind "Other tests (N)") and show their results as printed, without a chart.
- **Blood pressure chart.** In Journal, choose the Blood pressure chip. With readings in two or more weeks, the Blood pressure card shows the 7-day averages of the last 26 weeks as a chart (systolic solid, diastolic dashed). Tap or slide along it to read a week; the newest point is the card's 7-day average.

## Injection sites (dev build, first part)
- Tap an injectable dose on Today (or log an extra injection from Log) to open its sheet. A **Site** row sits above Time: the sites you used before plus the suggested next site, already selected, with "Last: L delt · Yesterday" under it. **More** shows all 12 sites (deltoid, pec, abdomen, ventrogluteal, glute and thigh, left and right); before any site is recorded the row shows **Choose site**.
- The suggestion follows your own order: the site that came after your last site the previous time you used it; the first time, the same spot on the other side. It is kept per compound.
- When the compound's last injection has no site, nothing is preselected. Tap the selected site to clear it. A skipped dose stores no site. Opening a taken dose again shows its site.

## Other changes
- Web app history import: bloodwork values entered in conventional units keep their exact value (cholesterol 200 mg/dL stays 200 and is no longer flagged high). Draws imported with v0.5.0-dev.2 keep their old values.
- The README and the v0.5.0-dev.2 notes point to the right page for the web app import (Settings › Export and data).

## Not yet
- The bloodwork import reads the answer only through **Paste answer**. Reading the copied answer when you return to the app, sharing it to the app and opening a file come later. Uncertain results are left out instead of asking.
- The site shows only in the dose sheet: the one-tap check on Today and the reminder's Taken do not record a site yet, and Journal and reports do not show it yet.

## Data
The database goes from version 3 to version 4: a migration adds the injection site to dose logs and keeps everything else as it is. The backup format stays `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
- Old backups restore in this build.
- A backup from this build restores in the regular app 0.4.0 only as far as that version understands it: it drops the injection sites and the parts of bloodwork it does not know (lab ranges, "<" and ">", names and units of other tests). The rest restores as with earlier dev builds.
