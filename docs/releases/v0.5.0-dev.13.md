Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.13.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Edit any logged dose:** tap a dose in Journal, or an extra dose on Today or in the day view, to open it in the dose sheet. You can change the amount, date, time, injection site, note, and Taken or Skipped for planned doses. Save offers Undo. This is also how you fix the site of a past dose.
- **Delete entry:** the blood pressure, note, symptom, bloodwork and dose edit sheets end with "Delete entry", with Undo. Long-press still works.
- **One Log menu:** Journal's + opens the same Log menu as Today, Extra dose included. New entries show "… saved" with Undo on both tabs.
- **One "Logged today":** Today lists extra doses and journal entries together by time. Every row opens its editor.
- **Journal dose lines** look like the other rows: an icon, the short name ("Test C"), the amount and the time.
- **Bloodwork sheet:** markers you have results for come first. With no results yet, hormones and blood count come first. The rest sit under "More markers".

## Data
No database or backup change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
