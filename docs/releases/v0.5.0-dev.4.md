Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.4.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

This release finishes injection sites: the suggested next site now shows on Today, in the dose reminder and in the Journal, and the one-tap check and the reminder's Taken record it.

## Injection sites (dev build)
1. **Log dose sheet.** Tap an injectable dose on Today (or log an extra injection from Log) to open its sheet. The **Site** row above Time shows the sites you used before plus the suggested next site, already selected, with "Last: L delt · Yesterday" under it. Tap another site to use it instead, **More** for all 12 sites (deltoid, pec, abdomen, ventrogluteal, glute and thigh, left and right), or the selected site to clear it. Before a compound has any site the row shows **Choose site**. **Log** records the site with the dose.
2. **Today.** A pending injectable row ends with the suggested site: "35.71 mg · 0.18 mL · pin 5/7 · R delt". Missed rows show it too. When a card has two pending doses of one compound, only the first shows a site. Taken rows show the site that was recorded; skipped rows show none.
3. **One tap.** Tap the check on a row: the dose is logged with the site the row showed, the message reads "Test C taken · R delt" ("Test C logged · R delt" on the Missed card), and the compound's next row moves on to the next site. **Log all** records each row's site in the same way.
4. **Reminder.** The line of an injectable in the dose reminder ends with the suggested site: "Test C (testosterone cypionate) · 100 mg · 0.5 mL · R VG". **Taken** (or **Take all**) records exactly the sites shown. When one reminder holds two doses of one compound (a late alarm), only the latest one shows and records a site. A reminder for a dose you already logged in the app changes nothing when you tap Taken. Snooze shows the site again when the reminder comes back.
5. **Journal.** A taken dose with a site reads "125 mg · 0.63 mL · R VG". Editing a dose in Journal keeps its site.

### How the next site is picked
- Per compound, from its last 24 taken doses. Skipped doses do not count.
- The suggestion is the site that came after your last site the previous time you used it, so the app follows your own order. Example: L delt, R delt, L VG, R VG, then L delt again: the next suggestion is R delt.
- When your last site has no earlier use in those 24 doses, the suggestion is the same spot on the other side (after L VG, R VG).

### How to change it
- **This dose only:** tap the row instead of the check and pick another site in the Log dose sheet.
- **Your order:** the rotation learns from what you record. If you pick L glute after L delt, the next time you are at L delt the suggestion is L glute.
- **Stop suggesting:** clear the site in the Log dose sheet (tap the selected site). Nothing is suggested until you pick a site again.

### What records no site
- A dose logged from the widget, or from a day opened with the calendar icon or the week bar (the Day sheet), records no site.
- When a compound's newest taken dose has no site, the suggestion stops: Today rows and the reminder show none and the Log dose sheet preselects none, until you pick a site in the Log dose sheet.
- Reminders shown before you updated to this build carry no site: their Taken records none. Log that dose from Today instead, or tap it on Today afterwards and pick its site.
- A skipped dose stores no site.

Tip: with a dose on the Missed card and today's dose of the same compound, check the Missed card first. Checked afterwards, the missed dose takes the next site at its earlier time, and the rotation can suggest a site again too soon.

## Other changes
- In the dev build, Today shows its dose rows once the whole dose history has loaded, so a row never appears first without its site (on a long history this can take a moment longer).
- The README's dev section describes sites on Today, the reminder and Journal, and what records no site.

## Not yet
- Reports (HTML and Markdown) do not print sites.
- A past dose's site cannot be changed or cleared: Journal's edit keeps it but has no Site row, and the Log dose sheet opens only today's doses. Picking the right site for the next dose puts the suggestion back on track; the old record stays.
- With two plan items of one injectable in the same part of the day, **Log all** records the site on the first row only and the second row gets none, so the suggestion stops. Pick the next site in the Log dose sheet once.
- The bloodwork import still reads the answer only through **Paste answer**.

## Data
The database stays at version 4, as in v0.5.0-dev.3: this build has no migration. Updating from v0.5.0-dev.2 or earlier runs the migration from version 3 to 4 that adds the injection site to dose logs and keeps everything else as it is. The backup format stays `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
- Old backups restore in this build.
- A backup from this build restores in the regular app 0.4.0 only as far as that version understands it: it drops the injection sites and the parts of bloodwork it does not know (lab ranges, "<" and ">", names and units of other tests). The rest restores as with earlier dev builds.
