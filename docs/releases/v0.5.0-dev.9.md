Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.9.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Time pickers follow your clock setting.** With the 12-hour clock (Settings › Units and formats) the dial shows AM and PM, so 11 PM is 11 and PM instead of 23.
- **Figures that fit:** the item editor shows "40 mg/day" on one line; a Plan card with three or more weekdays reads "5 days" (the editor lists them); the Symptoms mood row is two even rows of five.
- **Levels readings** drop the decimal from 100 up ("1214 ng/dL"), like the figures.
- **Restore** asks about "the backup from 2 Oct 2026" in your local date and format (it showed the UTC date, a day off after midnight) and counts "1 plan item".
- **Journal** shows no Adherence card while nothing is logged yet.

## For development
- An on-device smoke test (`SmokeTest`) opens every tab, sheet and settings page of the real app on an emulator and fails on any crash. It caught the Bloodwork crash of dev.1–dev.4 when that bug was put back on purpose. It runs before every tag.

## Data
No database or backup change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
