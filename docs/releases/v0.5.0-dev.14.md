Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.14.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Levels reading under the chart:** sliding on a chart shows a line and a dot instead of a bubble, so the curve and lab results stay visible. Under the chart: "Wed, Sep 30 10:55 AM · est. 799 ng/dL", your last dose by its short name, and the entries logged nearby. On the Testosterone chart a blood draw reads "Bloodwork · T 1100 ng/dL · E2 45 pg/mL". A "Lab result" legend explains the diamonds.
- **Compound editor:** the peak per mg is shown and entered in the curve's unit (for example ng/mL). Saving without changing it keeps the exact stored value.
- **Plan item editor:** the line about any-time reminders shows only for any-time items.
- **Settings:** shorter text for units, a plain "Time recorded when you check a dose", and a Reports text that names everything a report contains.
- **Bloodwork import:** a result printed as a word ("niet reactief") goes to the entry note. The AI report lists a draw's note lines separated by "; ".

## Data
No database or backup change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
