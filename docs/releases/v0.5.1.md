Fixes on top of 0.5.0. Install **ProtocolTracker-v0.5.1.apk** over 0.5.0; your data stays.

- **Going back with a swipe** no longer cuts off: the screen shrinks as you swipe and slides away when you let go. Swiping back in and letting go cancels.
- **Screen changes are seamless:** the old screen fades out before the new one fades in, so text never shows over other text (back button, opening screens and switching tabs).
- **"Skipped last time" / "Missed last time"** on a dose planned twice a day now refers to the same time of day: a skipped evening dose no longer shows on the next morning's dose.
