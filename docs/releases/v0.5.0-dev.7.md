Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.7.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Journal day headers** read "Today", "Yesterday" or "Thu, Sep 24" in your date format, with the year only for another year ("Tue, Oct 21 2025"). The repeated ISO date is gone.
- **Blood pressure card:** "Latest 123/83 mmHg" on its own line, the day and time under it, so the line never wraps after a dangling "·".
- **Today rows** no longer carry the INJ / ORAL / SUPPORT tag (the dose line says it; Plan keeps the tag), and a number never splits from its unit: "0.18 mL" or "pin 5/7" stay on one line, and lines break only after a "·".
- **Symptoms:** the sheet opens without advice; the groups are headed "Often listed with low estrogen", "Often listed with high estrogen" and "Other"; Journal and Today symptom lines show the names and mood without "1 low-E2 · 1 high-E2" counts.
- **Log menu:** Note reads "Anything else, in your own words"; Symptoms reads "Symptoms, mood and hair shedding".

## Data
No database or backup change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
