Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.10.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Plan cards** show per pin and volume in the band and list the days with the time of day underneath: "Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun · Morning". Schedules like "Every 3.5 days" appear once instead of twice.
- **Levels:** "Back to now" has a location icon (the calendar icon opens another day on Today); with nothing to plot, the screen offers **Open plan**.
- **Journal:** the Bloodwork row on All breaks only between its parts, never inside "1 out of range".
- **Web history import:** the confirm dialog is titled "Import CycleTracker history?".

## For development
- CI runs the on-device smoke test on an Android emulator for every push, and a release waits for it.
