Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.12.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Extra doses:** Log › Extra dose lists the compounds in your plan first ("In your plan"), and the form starts at the amount you last took, with "Last taken: 0.5 mg · Yesterday 9:00 PM" under the name.
- **Mood chart:** Journal › Symptoms shows your mood ratings of the last 90 days (up to the latest one) as dots on a 1–10 scale once you rated mood on two or more days. Tap a dot to read its day.
- **Imported symptoms** from the web app with names the app does not list read as names ("High E2, Bloating") instead of stored keys.

## Data
No database or backup change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.
