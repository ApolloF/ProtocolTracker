Dev pre-release. One APK:

- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.5.0-dev.15.apk** – the dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and updates an earlier dev build in place. The regular app stays at 0.4.0.

This is the last dev build before 0.5.0. Before installing 0.5.0, export a backup from this build: backups now include your settings.

## Changes (dev build)
- **Day starts at:** Settings › Times of day sets when a new day begins (midnight to 6:00, default 4:00). Until then Today still shows the previous day, so an evening dose taken at 1:00 counts for that evening. Journal lists late-night entries under the day before. Doses planned between midnight and the day start stay on their own date.
- **Week strip:** swipe it to earlier or later weeks (back to your first log or the plan start). Tapping a day shows that day below the strip, with a short slide, instead of opening a sheet. Past days can be checked off at their planned time; "Back to today" or the system back returns. Days show "1 miss", "1 skip", "all" or "1/3". Planned days before your first log show "–" and read "not logged" instead of missed.
- **Missed last time:** a dose due today says when the previous one was missed or skipped and when you last took the compound, for example "Missed last time (Thu 24 Sep) · last taken Mon 21 Sep, 9:00".
- **Skipped doses:** a skip stays on the day and time it was planned, never shows under "Logged late", shows no amount and keeps no site. Groups where everything was skipped say "Skipped". A taken dose can be changed to skipped in its edit sheet. The day summary and Journal adherence count skipped doses separately ("86% taken (6/7) · 1 skipped").
- **Reminders:** a reminder with several doses offers "Skip all", and a short silent note confirms a skip.
- **Back navigation:** the back animation runs its full length, also with the back gesture, and the bottom bar no longer resizes the screen while it plays.
- **Levels:** "Back to now" shows only after you move, zoom or read the chart.

## Data
Backups now include settings (restoring an older backup leaves your settings as they are). No database change: database version 4, backup format `protocoltracker-backup-2`.

With the new 4:00 day start, a "count from last dose" interval dose taken between midnight and 4:00 counts for the day before.
