Two APKs this time:

- **ProtocolTracker-v0.4.0.apk** – the regular app (updates 0.3.0 in place).
- **ProtocolTracker-Dev-v0.4.0.apk** – dev build. Installs next to the regular app with its own data and a purple icon, and adds symptom logging and bloodwork. Move data between them with Settings > Export and data > Save backup / Restore backup.

## Changes
- **Navigation bar:** the press highlight stays inside the tab's pill instead of a large round splash.
- **Animations:** new setting in Appearance (Full, Reduced, Off). Reduced, the default, uses short fades so screens respond at once. Screen changes no longer resize the content while it moves, which clipped screens before.
- **Units and formats:** new settings page: 12- or 24-hour clock, date order (26 Sep / Sep 26), conventional (ng/dL) or SI (nmol/L) units for level curves and lab results, and mL or U-100 syringe units for injection volumes. Reports keep ISO dates and mL.
- **Today:** days in the week strip open that day. Check off or backfill its doses (logged at the planned time), step to earlier or later days, or pick any date with the new calendar button.
- **Levels (experimental):** *Scrub level charts* in Settings > Experimental. Slide a finger along a chart to read the estimate at that moment and see the last dose, blood pressure and doses, notes and readings logged within two days (or the nearest one). Two fingers pan and zoom. Light vibration ticks per day and on each log, tuned per device (predefined effects on Samsung, haptic primitives where fully supported) and following the phone's touch vibration setting.

## Dev build only
- **Symptoms:** low- and high-estrogen signs and general symptoms from the CycleTracker web app, optional mood (1–10), hair shedding and note.
- **Bloodwork:** 25 markers (hormones, blood count, lipids, metabolic, kidney, liver, PSA) entered in either unit system. The Journal shows the latest result per marker with reference range and in/out of range; total testosterone results are drawn on the Testosterone curve.
- Both are in the Journal (filters, edit, delete), reports and backups.

## Data
Database version 3 (adds one column for symptom and bloodwork details). Existing data is migrated in place.
